Since this is a technical "Production Blueprint," the GTM (Go-To-Market) strategy should not be "salesy." Instead, it should be positioned as **Operational Transparency**. 

The goal is to move from "We are building a tool" to "We have a deterministic engine with a known gap."

### Part 1: Honest GTM Strategy

**Positioning:** *The Deterministic Resource Engine.*
Stop selling "AI Automation" (which sounds vague/unreliable) and start selling "Gated Resource Orchestration."

**The Value Prop:**
"Most AI agents guess. Sam-Harness v2 uses a gated N-Tier architecture. Every state transition (from Pending to Invoiced) is deterministic, audited, and requires a 'Go/No-Go' signal. We don't just automate tasks; we orchestrate physical and digital resources with a hard audit trail."

**The "Honest" Angle (Handling the Gap):**
Don't hide the missing Billing Engine. Use it as a roadmap milestone. 
*   *Current State:* Core Orchestration, Booking Primitives, and Security Framework are **Live/Verified**.
*   *Next Milestone:* Closing the loop with the Billing Engine to enable full revenue automation.

**Target Audience:**
Operations Managers or Technical Founders who have been burned by "hallucinating" AI agents and need a system that follows a strict state machine.
