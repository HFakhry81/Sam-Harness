Since this is a technical "Control Plane" (the brain/memory layer of an AI agency), the GTM should focus on **Reliability** and **Statefulness**. Most AI agents are "forgetful" (stateless); this system solves that.

### Part 1: Honest GTM Strategy

**The Core Value Proposition:**
*"Moving from 'Chatbots' to 'Systems of Record'."*

**The "Honest" Angle:**
Avoid claiming "Full Autonomy" or "AGI." Instead, frame it as **Infrastructure**. You aren't selling a magic bot; you are selling a **Management Layer** that ensures AI agents don't lose track of project goals, daily tasks, or delivery artifacts.

**Target Audience:**
*   Technical Founders / Agency Owners.
*   Operations Managers overseeing AI workflows.
*   Developers struggling with "context drift" in LLM chains.

**Key Messaging Pillars:**
1.  **Persistence:** No more repeating instructions. The `project-memory.json` acts as the agency's long-term memory.
2.  **Auditability:** The `Daily Ops Ledger` means you can actually see what the agent did, rather than guessing based on a chat history.
3.  **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** The UI isn't for the AI; it's for the human to approve the "Delivery Pack" before it hits the client.
