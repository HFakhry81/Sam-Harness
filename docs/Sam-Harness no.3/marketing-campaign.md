### 1. Honest GTM Strategy

**The Core Problem:** Most AI agents are "black boxes." They do work, but you don't know *how* they reached a decision, and they forget the context the moment the session ends.

**The Solution (The Control Plane):** We are implementing a three-tier stability layer:
1.  **Project Memory:** So the AI doesn't ask the same questions twice.
2.  **Ops Ledger:** An immutable "black box flight recorder" for every action taken.
3.  **Report Viewer:** A transparent window into the AI's logic.

**Value Proposition:** 
*"Reliability through Traceability."* We aren't just building a tool that works; we are building a tool that can prove **why** it worked.

**Target Audience:** Technical stakeholders who are tired of "AI hallucinations" and need an audit trail for production-grade deployments.
